> In light of the common-law history of noncompetition clauses in restraint of trade, Section 9-08-06, N.D.C.C., Olson v. Swendiman, supra; Spectrum Emergency Care, Inc. v. St. Joseph’s Hospital & Health Center, supra; and California decisions, such as Buskuhl v. Family Life Ins. Co., supra, construing the same Field Code provision as § 9-08-06, N.D.C.C., we conclude that Paragraph 12(D) of the parties’ contract constitutes an unlawful restraint of trade and is void under § 9-08-06, N.D.C.C.

> Apparently, the employer is more concerned about Strauman’s knowledge of the intricacies of their business operation. However, absent any wrongdoing, we cannot agree that Strauman should be prohibited from utilizing his knowledge and talents in this área (see Restatement, Agency 2d, § 396, Comment b).

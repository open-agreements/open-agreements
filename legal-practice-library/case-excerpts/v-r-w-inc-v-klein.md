### v-r-w-inc-v-klein-it-is-well-established-that-a-0a963823 {#v-r-w-inc-v-klein-it-is-well-established-that-a-0a963823}

> It is well established that a conveyance by one tenant, to which the other has not consented, cannot bind the entire fee or impair the nonconsenting spouse’s survivorship interest (Hiles v Fisher, supra; Lawriw v City of Rochester, 14 AD2d 13, affd 11 NY2d 759).

### v-r-w-inc-v-klein-nonetheless-the-grantee-s-or-mortgagee-s-101e7e83 {#v-r-w-inc-v-klein-nonetheless-the-grantee-s-or-mortgagee-s-101e7e83}

### v-r-w-inc-v-klein-since-the-status-of-a-tenant-dffc1408 {#v-r-w-inc-v-klein-since-the-status-of-a-tenant-dffc1408}

> Since the status of a tenant by the entirety is reserved exclusively to those co-owners who are married to each other, the interest acquired by a grantee or mortgagee of such a unilateral conveyance is not denominated a tenancy by the entirety, but rather is labeled a tenancy in common.

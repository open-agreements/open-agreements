### holiday-v-poffenbarger-a-defendant-may-be-liable-for-fbe15c6a {#holiday-v-poffenbarger-a-defendant-may-be-liable-for-fbe15c6a}

> A defendant may be liable for injuries caused by an intoxicated guest that occurred on the defendant’s property, or in an area under the defendant’s control, where the defendant had the opportunity to control the intoxicated guest and was reasonably aware of the need for such control (see D’Amico v Christie, 71 NY2d 76, 85 [1987]).

### holiday-v-poffenbarger-here-the-sigma-pi-defendants-established-e7637153 {#holiday-v-poffenbarger-here-the-sigma-pi-defendants-established-e7637153}

> Here, the Sigma Pi defendants established their prima facie entitlement to judgment as a matter of law dismissing the negligence cause of action insofar as asserted against SPFI by showing that the plaintiffs injuries occurred in an area not under SPFI’s control and, thus, that SPFI had no duty to supervise or control Poffenbarger’s conduct in that area (see Walters v Sternlieb, 255 AD2d 309, 310 [1998]; White v Celebrity Lounge, 215 AD2d 650 [1995]).

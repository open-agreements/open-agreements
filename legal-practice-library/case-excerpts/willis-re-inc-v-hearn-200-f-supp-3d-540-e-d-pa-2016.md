### willis-re-inc-v-hearn-200-f-supp-3d-540-e-d-pa-2016-if-hearn-retired-from-willis-re-c7f17b81 {#willis-re-inc-v-hearn-200-f-supp-3d-540-e-d-pa-2016-if-hearn-retired-from-willis-re-c7f17b81}

> If Hearn “retired” from Willis Re, he has no obligation to repay incentive awards; if Hearn did not “retire” from Willis Re, he is obligated to repay a portion of incentive awards. We find Hearn did not “retire” under the terms of the parties’ agreements.

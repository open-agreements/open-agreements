# Dallenbach v. MAPCO Gas Products, Inc.

### dallenbach-v-mapco-gas-products-inc-dallenbach-s-annual-bonus-is-wages-the-56a1a114 {#dallenbach-v-mapco-gas-products-inc-dallenbach-s-annual-bonus-is-wages-the-56a1a114}

> Dallenbach’s annual bonus is wages. The bonus clearly was part of the compensation owed him for his labor or services, and its method of determination falls within the definition as an “other basis of calculation.” The bonus was not a gift; MAPCO Gas was contractually bound to pay it.

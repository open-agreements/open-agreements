### kalb-voorhis-co-v-american-financial-corp-2d-cir-1993-the-law-of-the-state-of-28891fc4 {#kalb-voorhis-co-v-american-financial-corp-2d-cir-1993-the-law-of-the-state-of-28891fc4}

> The law of the state of incorporation determines when the corporate form will be disregarded and liability will be imposed on shareholders: “Because a corporation is a creature of state law whose primary purpose is to insulate shareholders from legal liability, the state of incorporation has the greater interest in determining when and if that insulation is to be stripped away.”

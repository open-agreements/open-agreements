### nps-llc-v-minihane-it-is-the-defendant-s-burden-to-cdb1aa32 {#nps-llc-v-minihane-it-is-the-defendant-s-burden-to-cdb1aa32}

> It is the defendant’s burden to show that the amount of liquidated damages is “unreasonably and grossly disproportionate to the real damages from a breach” or “unconscionably excessive.”

> Where damages are easily ascertainable, and the amount provided for is grossly disproportionate to actual damages or unconscionably excessive, the court will award the aggrieved party no more than its actual damages.

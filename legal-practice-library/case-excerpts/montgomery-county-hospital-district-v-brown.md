### montgomery-county-hospital-district-v-brown-for-such-a-contract-to-exist-9f4a3f11 {#montgomery-county-hospital-district-v-brown-for-such-a-contract-to-exist-9f4a3f11}

> For such a contract to exist, the employer must unequivocally indicate a definite intent to be bound not to terminate the employee except under clearly specified circumstances. General comments that an employee will not be discharged as long as his work is satisfactory do not in themselves manifest such an intent. Neither do statements that an employee will be discharged only for “good reason” or “good cause” when there is no agreement on what those terms encompass.

### nanomech-inc-v-suresh-under-the-plain-language-of-the-9a32aebe {#nanomech-inc-v-suresh-under-the-plain-language-of-the-9a32aebe}

> Under the plain language of the agreement, Suresh would be prohibited from working for any company that is a competitor of NanoMech, in any capacity, anywhere in the world. Even though NanoMech’s proprietary interests warrant protection, the leading Arkansas authorities suggest that Suresh’s noncompete agreement unduly infringes on her ability to pursue work in her chosen field, and is therefore overbroad.

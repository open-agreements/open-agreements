> In this opinion, we clarify that NRS 608.140 explicitly recognizes a private cause of action for unpaid wages. Accordingly, we conclude that NRS Chapter 608 provides a private right of action for unpaid wages. Because petitioner's claims were for unpaid wages under NRS 608.016 (payment for each hour worked), NRS 608.018 (payment for overtime), and NRS 608.020 through NRS 608.050 (payment upon termination), we grant the petition for extraordinary relief.

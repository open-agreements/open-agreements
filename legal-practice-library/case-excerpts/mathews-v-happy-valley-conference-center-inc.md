### mathews-v-happy-valley-conference-center-inc-because-we-have-already-determined-that-5b3f13f5 {#mathews-v-happy-valley-conference-center-inc-because-we-have-already-determined-that-5b3f13f5}

> Because we have already determined that defendants (as a single employer) were plaintiff’s employer, they fall under the “employer” category for purposes of subdivision (h). And as employers that are religious entities, they are exempt from FEHA liability.

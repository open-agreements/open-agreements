> But, under section 2804, the existence of an agreement between an employer or employee regarding a lump-sum reimbursement payment would not relieve the employer of the statutory obligation to pay full reimbursement, nor would it bar an employee challenge to a lump-sum payment as being insufficient under section 2802 to provide full reimbursement.

### gattuso-v-harte-hanks-shoppers-inc-42-cal-4th-554-2007-we-conclude-that-an-employer-may-1aaeb040 {#gattuso-v-harte-hanks-shoppers-inc-42-cal-4th-554-2007-we-conclude-that-an-employer-may-1aaeb040}

> We conclude that an employer may satisfy its statutory reimbursement obligation by paying employees enhanced compensation in the form of increases in base salary or increases in commission rates, or both, provided there is a means or method to apportion the enhanced compensation to determine what amount is being paid for labor performed and what amount is reimbursement for business expenses.

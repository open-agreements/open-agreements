### marshall-v-safeway-inc-437-md-542-2014-when-an-employer-makes-an-unauthorized-e7e8be22 {#marshall-v-safeway-inc-437-md-542-2014-when-an-employer-makes-an-unauthorized-e7e8be22}

> When an employer makes an unauthorized deduction under § 3-503, it is not paying all the compensation that is due to the employee. It is paying some part of that compensation (or perhaps all of it), unlawfully, to someone else. A violation of § 3-503 necessarily constitutes a violation also of § 3-502 or § 3-505.

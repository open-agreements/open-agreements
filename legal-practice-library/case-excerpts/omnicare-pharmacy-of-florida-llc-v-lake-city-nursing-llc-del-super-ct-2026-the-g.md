> As a general rule, a buyer of assets is not liable for a seller’s debts or tortious conduct.48 However, this general rule does not apply where (i) the buyer assumed the seller’s liability; (ii) there is a de facto merger or consolidation; (iii) the buyer is a mere continuation of the seller under a different name; or (iv) there was fraud in the transaction.

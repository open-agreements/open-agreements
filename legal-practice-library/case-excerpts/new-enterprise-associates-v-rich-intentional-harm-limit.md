> The Covenant is not unreasonable on the facts of this case. Sophisticated repeat players consented explicitly to a clear provision in a stockholder-level agreement that applies only to a specific transaction. Nevertheless, the Covenant cannot relieve the defendants of tort liability for intentional harm.

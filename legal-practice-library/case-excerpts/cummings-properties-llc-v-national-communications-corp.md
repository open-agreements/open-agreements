### cummings-properties-llc-v-national-communications-corp-if-at-the-time-the-contract-b566a3af {#cummings-properties-llc-v-national-communications-corp-if-at-the-time-the-contract-b566a3af}

> If, at the time the contract was made, actual damages were difficult to ascertain and the sum agreed on by the parties as liquidated damages represents a reasonable forecast of damages expected to occur in the event of a breach, it will usually be enforced.

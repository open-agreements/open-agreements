### new-jersey-transit-authority-v-new-jersey-transit-pba-314-n-j-super-129-app-div--the-new-jersey-public-employment-b2c3a7bf {#new-jersey-transit-authority-v-new-jersey-transit-pba-314-n-j-super-129-app-div--the-new-jersey-public-employment-b2c3a7bf}

> The New Jersey Public Employment Relations Commission (Commission) granted summary judgment to Local 304 reasoning that compensation issues and training costs are mandatorily negotiable; that being required to repay training costs upon leaving employment is a term and condition of employment affecting the employees’ compensation and duration of employment; and that the repayment requirement is negotiable despite the fact that employees do not have to repay until after leaving employment.

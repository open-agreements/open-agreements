### christopher-v-tomeck-as-the-final-transfer-of-funds-aa00a001 {#christopher-v-tomeck-as-the-final-transfer-of-funds-aa00a001}

> As the final transfer of funds to the trust from decedent occurred in 2001 and plaintiff was notified of that transfer in February 2002, plaintiffs claims asserted in June 2008 based on fraud, Debtor and Creditor Law §§ 273, 274, 275 and 276 and EPTL 7-3.1 are all time-barred (see CPLR 213 [8]; Fiber Consultants, Inc. v Fiber Optek Interconnect Corp., 56 AD3d 605, 605 [2008]; Ehrler v Cataffo, 42 AD3d 424, 425 [2007]; Durazinski v Chandler, 41 AD3d 918, 919 [2007]; State of New York v Cetero, 233 AD2d 580, 582 [1996]).

### christopher-v-tomeck-indeed-plaintiff-s-claims-against-the-847bf2c3 {#christopher-v-tomeck-indeed-plaintiff-s-claims-against-the-847bf2c3}

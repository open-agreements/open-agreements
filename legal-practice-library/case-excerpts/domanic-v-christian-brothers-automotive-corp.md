### domanic-v-christian-brothers-automotive-corp-christian-brothers-automotive-corporatio-fa2c7e91 {#domanic-v-christian-brothers-automotive-corp-christian-brothers-automotive-corporatio-fa2c7e91}

> Christian Brothers Automotive Corporation is a faith-based auto- repair business that franchises only to Christians. All of its more than 250 franchisees—a group which includes some ethnic Jews—are professing Christians. When the company refused to franchise to Evan Domanic, who is an ethnic and religious Jew, Domanic sued for racial discrimination under 42 U.S.C. § 1981.

### nelson-v-eighth-judicial-district-court-therefore-contrary-to-eric-s-assertions-288d842c {#nelson-v-eighth-judicial-district-court-therefore-contrary-to-eric-s-assertions-288d842c}

> Therefore, contrary to Eric's assertions, we did not determine that all assets in the trusts were separate property. Rather, our mandate in Klabacka, that the district court trace trust assets, demonstrates that at the time of the divorce decree, the LSN and ELN Trusts may have included property with a claim of community interest to which the JPI should extend. Accordingly, the district court must impose a JPI over all trust property with a claim of community interest.

### capistrant-v-lifetouch-national-school-studios-inc-916-n-w-2d-23-minn-2018-this-case-is-not-about-liquidated-38351f7c {#capistrant-v-lifetouch-national-school-studios-inc-916-n-w-2d-23-minn-2018-this-case-is-not-about-liquidated-38351f7c}

> This case is not about liquidated damages because Lifetouch does not contend that the residual commission amount forfeited by Capistrant's breach accurately represents the damages caused by Capistrant's failure to return its property. The liquidated-damages or penalty analysis therefore is not applicable to the forfeiture clause at issue in this case.

> We agree with the court of appeals that in the circumstances of this case, the guidance provided by section 229 of the Restatement is consistent with our precedent that disfavors forfeitures.

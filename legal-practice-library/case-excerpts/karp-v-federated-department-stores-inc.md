# Karp v. Federated Department Stores, Inc.

### karp-v-federated-department-stores-inc-contrary-to-flowerama-s-contention-1a727d5f {#karp-v-federated-department-stores-inc-contrary-to-flowerama-s-contention-1a727d5f}

> Contrary to Flowerama’s contention, General Obligations Law § 5-321 is inapplicable because the agreement between it and Federated was not a lease, but was, as it was called, a license agreement. Pursuant to the agreement’s terms, Flowerama did not have exclusive control and dominion over a defined space.

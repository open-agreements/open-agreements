### knutton-v-cofield-273-n-c-355-1968-a-stipulated-sum-is-for-liquidated-be400990 {#knutton-v-cofield-273-n-c-355-1968-a-stipulated-sum-is-for-liquidated-be400990}

> [A] stipulated sum is for liquidated damages only (1) where the damages which the parties might reasonably anticipate are difficult to ascertain because of their indefiniteness or uncertainty and (2) where the amount stipulated is either a reasonable estimate of the damages which would probably be caused by a breach or is reasonably proportionate to the damages which have actually been caused by the breach.

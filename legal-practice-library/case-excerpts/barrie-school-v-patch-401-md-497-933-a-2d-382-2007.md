### barrie-school-v-patch-401-md-497-933-a-2d-382-2007-there-are-three-essential-elements-of-affdcb30 {#barrie-school-v-patch-401-md-497-933-a-2d-382-2007-there-are-three-essential-elements-of-affdcb30}

> There are three essential elements of a valid and enforceable liquidated damages clause. First, such a clause must provide in clear and unambiguous terms for a certain sum. Secondly, the liquidated damages must reasonably be compensation for the damages anticipated by the breach. Thirdly, liquidated damage clauses are by their nature mandatory binding agreements before the fact which may not be altered to correspond to actual damages determined after the fact.

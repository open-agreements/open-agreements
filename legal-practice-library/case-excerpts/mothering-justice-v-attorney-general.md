### mothering-justice-v-attorney-general-we-hold-that-this-decision-to-c1a2a84c {#mothering-justice-v-attorney-general-we-hold-that-this-decision-to-c1a2a84c}

> We hold that this decision to adopt the initiatives and then later amend them in the same legislative session (what has been referred to as “adopt-and-amend”) violated the people’s constitutionally guaranteed right to propose and enact laws through the initiative process. We therefore reverse the Court of Appeals’ judgment and reinstate 2018 PA 337 and 2018 PA 338 as set forth in this opinion.

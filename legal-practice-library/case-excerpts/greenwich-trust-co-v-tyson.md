### greenwich-trust-co-v-tyson-the-attempt-of-a-man-to-24704c7d {#greenwich-trust-co-v-tyson-the-attempt-of-a-man-to-24704c7d}

> The attempt of a man to place his property in trust for his own benefit under limitations similar to those which characterize a spendthrift trust is a departure from the underlying basis for the creation of such trusts. That aside, the public policy which sustains such trusts when created for the benefit of another is, where the settlor is himself the beneficiary, overborne by other considerations.

### greenwich-trust-co-v-tyson-the-public-policy-which-subjects-to-abc19ad4 {#greenwich-trust-co-v-tyson-the-public-policy-which-subjects-to-abc19ad4}

### waffle-house-inc-v-williams-in-sexual-harassment-cases-an-employer-151f31b0 {#waffle-house-inc-v-williams-in-sexual-harassment-cases-an-employer-151f31b0}

> In sexual-harassment cases, an employer is entitled to an affirmative defense if it takes prompt remedial action to stop the alleged harassment. Specifically, Waffle House is entitled to a defense if (1) it exercised reasonable care to prevent and promptly correct any sexually harassing behavior, and (2) Williams unreasonably failed to take advantage of any preventive or corrective opportunities provided by Waffle House or to avoid harm otherwise.

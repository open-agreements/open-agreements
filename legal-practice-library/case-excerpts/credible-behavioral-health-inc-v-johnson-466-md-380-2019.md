### credible-behavioral-health-inc-v-johnson-466-md-380-2019-i-if-you-terminate-employment-with-eafd1560 {#credible-behavioral-health-inc-v-johnson-466-md-380-2019-i-if-you-terminate-employment-with-eafd1560}

> (i) If you terminate employment with the Company within 12 months following achievement of the degree, 100% of the Loan; (ii) If you terminate employment with the Company after the 12 month anniversary but on or before the 24 month anniversary following achievement of the degree, 75% of the Loan; (iii) If you terminate employment with the Company after the 24 month anniversary but on or before the 36 month anniversary following achievement of the degree, 50% of the Loan; or (iv) If you terminate employment with the Company after the 36 month anniversary following achievement of the degree, 0% of the Loan.

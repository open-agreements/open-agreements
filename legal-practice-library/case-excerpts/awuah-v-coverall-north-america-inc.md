### awuah-v-coverall-north-america-inc-chargebacks-represent-a-means-for-7594b61e {#awuah-v-coverall-north-america-inc-chargebacks-represent-a-means-for-7594b61e}

> “Chargebacks” represent a means for Coverall to recapture wages already earned and paid. They are not a valid setoff; they correspond to no “clear and established debt owed to the employer by the employee.”

### awuah-v-coverall-north-america-inc-if-an-employee-s-work-is-inadequate-19e54992 {#awuah-v-coverall-north-america-inc-if-an-employee-s-work-is-inadequate-19e54992}

> If an employee’s work is inadequate, Coverall is free to implement sanctions, including termination; Coverall is not free to withhold — much less recapture — the employee’s earned wages.

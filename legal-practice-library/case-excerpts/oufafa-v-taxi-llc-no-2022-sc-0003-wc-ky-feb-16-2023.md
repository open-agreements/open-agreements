### oufafa-v-taxi-llc-no-2022-sc-0003-wc-ky-feb-16-2023-the-fundamental-inquiry-in-both-ratliff-2405a1e3 {#oufafa-v-taxi-llc-no-2022-sc-0003-wc-ky-feb-16-2023-the-fundamental-inquiry-in-both-ratliff-2405a1e3}

> The fundamental inquiry in both Ratliff and Jani-King is the same: “In assessing the true nature of the parties’ relationship, courts must look at the practical, not just contractual, realities of the relationship.” Id. at 80. Importantly, however, the “central question” to the economic realities test is “the worker’s economic dependence upon the business for which he is laboring,” an inquiry not specifically captured under the ALJ’s in-depth analysis nor under our prior caselaw. Id. at 74 (citation omitted).

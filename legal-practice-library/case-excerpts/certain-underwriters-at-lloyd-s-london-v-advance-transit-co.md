### certain-underwriters-at-lloyd-s-london-v-advance-transit-co-based-upon-the-plain-and-ordinary-81c8f671 {#certain-underwriters-at-lloyd-s-london-v-advance-transit-co-based-upon-the-plain-and-ordinary-81c8f671}

> Based upon the plain and ordinary meaning of the term “however” within Insurance Law § 3420(a)(5), a claims-made policy can set a definite time frame for reporting claims, irrespective of prejudice, which can include “the policy period, any renewal thereof, or any extended reporting period.”

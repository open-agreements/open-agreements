### city-of-creston-v-barney-the-agreement-provided-for-the-following-4856272f {#city-of-creston-v-barney-the-agreement-provided-for-the-following-4856272f}

> The Agreement provided for the following sliding scale of reimbursement: (1) less than six months following the completion of training, one hundred percent reimbursement; (2) more than six months but less than one year following completion of training, seventy-five percent reimbursement; (3) more than one year but less than two years following completion of training, fifty percent reimbursement; and (4) more than two years but less than three years following completion of training, twenty-five percent reimbursement.

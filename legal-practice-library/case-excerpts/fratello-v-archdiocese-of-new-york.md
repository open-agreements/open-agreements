### fratello-v-archdiocese-of-new-york-in-sum-then-we-conclude-that-444660f6 {#fratello-v-archdiocese-of-new-york-in-sum-then-we-conclude-that-444660f6}

> In sum, then, we conclude that although Fratello’s formal title was not inherently religious, the record makes clear that she held herself out as a spiritual leader of the School and performed many important religious functions to advance its Roman Catholic mission. The ministerial exception thus bars her employment-discrimination claims because she was a minister within the meaning of the exception.

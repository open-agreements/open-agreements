### jennings-v-minco-technology-labs-inc-jennings-may-consent-or-not-to-d0dd1ca0 {#jennings-v-minco-technology-labs-inc-jennings-may-consent-or-not-to-d0dd1ca0}

> Jennings may consent or not to any future requirement that she give a urine sample or participate in a rehabilitation program, should either be asked of her; or she may reject further employment on the modified terms proposed by the company. She may not, however, determine unilaterally what the terms of her employment shall be and compel the company to contract with her on that basis against its will.

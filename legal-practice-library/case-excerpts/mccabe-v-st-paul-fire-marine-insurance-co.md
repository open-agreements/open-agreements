### mccabe-v-st-paul-fire-marine-insurance-co-we-further-conclude-that-plaintiffs-gave-21eadc6f {#mccabe-v-st-paul-fire-marine-insurance-co-we-further-conclude-that-plaintiffs-gave-21eadc6f}

> We further conclude that plaintiffs gave defendant notice of their claim against Fretz as soon as was reasonably possible, and thus that their failure to give notice to defendant during the policy period or extended reporting period did not invalidate their claim (see Insurance Law § 3420 [a] [4]; Wraight v Exchange Ins. Co. [appeal No. 2], 234 AD2d 916 , 917 [1996], lv denied 89 NY2d 813 [1997]). Contrary to defendant’s contention, Insurance Law § 3420 (a) (3) and (4) do not include exceptions for claims-made insurance policies.

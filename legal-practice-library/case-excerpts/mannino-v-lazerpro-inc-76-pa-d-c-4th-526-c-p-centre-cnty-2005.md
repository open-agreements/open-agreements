> Defendant further requests liquidated damages in the amount of $8,250 based upon plaintiff’s failure to remain employed for the full two-year commitment. Pursuant to attachment A, subsection C, of the employment contract, defendant is entitled to liquidated damages equivalent to three months salary if the breach occurs after the employee’s first year of employment.

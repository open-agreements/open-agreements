# AirTran New York, LLC v. Midwest Air Group, Inc.

### airtran-new-york-llc-v-midwest-air-group-inc-since-business-corporation-law-1312-041c498a {#airtran-new-york-llc-v-midwest-air-group-inc-since-business-corporation-law-1312-041c498a}

> Since Business Corporation Law § 1312 constitutes a statutory barrier to the foreign corporation’s right to bring suit, the party seeking to impose the barrier, in order to rebut the presumption that the corporation does business in its state of incorporation rather than New York, has the burden of proving that the foreign corporation’s activity in New York is systematic and regular (Alicanto, S. A. v Woolverton, 129 AD2d 601, 602 [1987]; see Acno-Tec Ltd. v Wall St. Suites, L.L.C., 24 AD3d 392 [2005]).

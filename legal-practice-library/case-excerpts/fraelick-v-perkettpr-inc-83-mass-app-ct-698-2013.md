### fraelick-v-perkettpr-inc-83-mass-app-ct-698-2013-fairly-read-the-complaint-alleges-that-21950411 {#fraelick-v-perkettpr-inc-83-mass-app-ct-698-2013-fairly-read-the-complaint-alleges-that-21950411}

> Fairly read, the complaint alleges that the otherwise permissible expense reimbursement arrangement designed to benefit employees was abandoned and replaced with a policy and practice which required the employee, under penalty of discharge, to advance, indefinitely, expenses for the employer’s benefit. Viewed in the light of Camara and Awuah, supra, this was a sufficient allegation of “reasonable belief” under Smith v. Winter Place LLC, 447 Mass. at 367 , to withstand a rule 12(b)(6) motion on the retaliation claim.

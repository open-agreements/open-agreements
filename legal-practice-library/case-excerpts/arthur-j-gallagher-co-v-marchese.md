### arthur-j-gallagher-co-v-marchese-an-employer-s-interests-justifying-a-63ea9cd8 {#arthur-j-gallagher-co-v-marchese-an-employer-s-interests-justifying-a-63ea9cd8}

> An employer’s interests justifying a restrictive covenant are limited “to the protection against misappropriation of the employer’s trade secrets or of confidential customer lists, or protection from competition by a former employee whose services are unique or extraordinary” (BDO Seidman v Hirshberg, 93 NY2d at 389 ; see Reed, Roberts Assoc. v Strauman, 40 NY2d at 308 ). In addition, “[t]he employer has a legitimate interest in preventing former employees from exploiting or appropriating the goodwill of a client or customer, which had been created and maintained at the employer’s expense, to the employer’s competitive detriment”

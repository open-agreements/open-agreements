### jeffcoat-v-state-department-of-labor-732-p-2d-1073-alaska-1987-alaska-has-not-however-directly-da29b468 {#jeffcoat-v-state-department-of-labor-732-p-2d-1073-alaska-1987-alaska-has-not-however-directly-da29b468}

> Alaska has not, however, directly addressed the distinction between employee and independent contractor for purposes of Alaska’s counterpart to the FLSA. We must turn to federal authorities for appropriate case law. The focal inquiry is whether the worker whose status is in question is within the class of persons meant to be protected by the Act. The court must determine whether the worker is dependent upon finding employment in the business of others. If the facts show such a dependency, the worker is an employee.

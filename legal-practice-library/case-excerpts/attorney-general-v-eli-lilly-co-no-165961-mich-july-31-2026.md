### attorney-general-v-eli-lilly-co-no-165961-mich-july-31-2026-we-hold-that-smith-and-liss-e1f4b400 {#attorney-general-v-eli-lilly-co-no-165961-mich-july-31-2026-we-hold-that-smith-and-liss-e1f4b400}

> We hold that Smith and Liss were wrongly decided because they improperly broadened the scope of the exemption, contrary to the plain language, structure, and purpose of the statute. And following our stare decisis principles, we overrule Smith and Liss. We hold that the MCL 445.904(1)(a) exemption should be applied narrowly in cases where the specific transaction or conduct at issue is authorized by law.

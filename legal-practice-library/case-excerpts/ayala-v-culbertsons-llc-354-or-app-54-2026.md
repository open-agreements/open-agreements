### ayala-v-culbertsons-llc-354-or-app-54-2026-the-fact-that-the-deductions-ultimately-aa223136 {#ayala-v-culbertsons-llc-354-or-app-54-2026-the-fact-that-the-deductions-ultimately-aa223136}

> The fact that the deductions ultimately returned money to defendant does not change the analysis. The ques- tion under subsection (3)(b) is whether the deduction was for the employee’s benefit. The undisputed facts establish that plaintiff received the money before the deduction and that he would not have received it without agreeing to the repay- ment arrangement.

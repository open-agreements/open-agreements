### state-ex-rel-department-of-labor-industrial-services-v-hill-the-general-test-of-an-employee-employer-470a0f3a {#state-ex-rel-department-of-labor-industrial-services-v-hill-the-general-test-of-an-employee-employer-470a0f3a}

> The general test of an employee/employer relationship is the right to control work. If the employer retains the right to control and to direct the activities of the employee in the details of work performed, and to determine the hours to be spent and the times to start and stop the work, the person performing work will be deemed an employee.

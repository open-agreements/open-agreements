### matter-of-antique-world-inc-tax-appeals-tribunal-while-shopping-and-browsing-may-not-7f9357b7 {#matter-of-antique-world-inc-tax-appeals-tribunal-while-shopping-and-browsing-may-not-7f9357b7}

> While shopping and browsing may not be commonly understood to be forms of amusement and entertainment, we believe that live entertainment, interesting demonstrations, an antique car show or a dance are forms of amusement and entertainment and a place offering them meets the definition of a "place of amusement" pursuant to Tax Law § 1101(d)(10).

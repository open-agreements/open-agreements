# Us

## Documents

* [Employee Expense Reimbursement in California](california.md) - California Labor Code sections 2802 and 2804 govern necessary business expenses, reimbursement agreements, and recovery of unpaid costs.
* [Employee Expense Reimbursement in Illinois](illinois.md) - Illinois law requires reimbursement of authorized necessary work expenses and sets rules for written policies, documentation, and submission time.
* [Employee Expense Reimbursement in Iowa](iowa.md) - Iowa Code § 91A.3 requires payment of authorized employee expenses and sets the timing for reimbursement and written refusals.
* [Employee Expense Reimbursement in Montana](montana.md) - Mont. Code Ann. § 39-2-701 covers necessary job expenses, ordinary business risks, employer-caused losses, remote-work costs, and the limits of the statutory text on waiver.
* [Employee Expense Reimbursement in New Hampshire](new-hampshire.md) - RSA 275:57 requires reimbursement of qualifying employer-requested expenses and sets a 30-day deadline and remedies.
* [Employee Expense Reimbursement in North Dakota](north-dakota.md) - North Dakota Century Code chapter 34-02 covers necessary job expenses, shared-use equipment, and limits on employer liability.
* [Employee Expense Reimbursement in South Dakota](south-dakota.md) - South Dakota's employee indemnity statutes cover necessary job expenses, exclude certain losses, and leave remote-work costs and waiver unaddressed expressly.

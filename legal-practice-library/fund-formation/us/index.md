# Us

## Documents

* [Hedge Fund Formation: The Limited Partnership Agreement](hedge-fund-limited-partnership-agreement.md) - A plain-language guide for hedge fund managers, and for the smaller investors they admit, to a U.S. hedge fund's limited partnership agreement, covering its purpose and parties, performance fees, hurdles and high-water marks, lock-ups and withdrawals, capital accounts, manager duties and protections, expenses, side letters, amendment, and dissolution.
* [Hedge Fund Formation: The Private Placement Memorandum](hedge-fund-private-placement-memorandum.md) - A plain-language guide for hedge fund managers, and for the smaller investors they admit, to a U.S. hedge fund's private placement memorandum, covering what the memorandum is, matching it to the limited partnership agreement, strategy, risk factors, conflicts of interest, service providers, taxes, retirement-plan investors, and subscriptions.
* [Hedge Fund Formation: Adviser Registration, Offering Exemptions, and Fund Documents](hedge-funds.md) - A plain-language guide for hedge fund managers, and for the smaller investors they admit, to forming a U.S. hedge fund, covering adviser registration, managers outside the United States, who can be charged a performance fee, Rule 506(b) and 506(c) offerings, the Section 3(c)(1) and 3(c)(7) exclusions, anti-fraud rules, and Form D, with separate guides to the limited partnership agreement and the private placement memorandum.

# Fund Formation

## Documents

* [Hedge Fund Limited Partnership Agreement Reviewer Checklist](hedge-fund-limited-partnership-agreement.md) - A clause-by-clause reviewer checklist for a hedge fund limited partnership agreement, a hedge fund here meaning an open-end private investment fund organized as a Delaware limited partnership and relying on Section 3(c)(1), written first for the manager setting the terms and second for a smaller investor signing without counsel, in the order that matters to both: what the manager charges, how and when money can come back out, how capital and gains are accounted for, what the manager may do and when it answers for it, costs and reporting, and how the agreement can be changed or the fund wound up. ([JSON](hedge-fund-limited-partnership-agreement.json))
* [Hedge Fund Private Placement Memorandum Reviewer Checklist](hedge-fund-private-placement-memorandum.md) - A section-by-section reviewer checklist for a hedge fund private placement memorandum, a hedge fund here meaning an open-end private investment fund organized as a Delaware limited partnership and relying on Section 3(c)(1): the summary of principal terms, what the investor actually pays, how and when money comes back, the offering exemption and investor-eligibility gates, strategy and risk disclosure, conflicts of interest, valuation and reporting, and the tax, ERISA, and anti-money-laundering disclosures. ([JSON](hedge-fund-private-placement-memorandum.json))

# Asset Purchase Reviewer Checklist[^about]

Reviewer checks for the asset sale, its optional terms and the conditions that make each safeguard relevant.

The checklist reads an asset purchase agreement as a contract under the state law the parties choose, together with the tax-reporting and successor-liability questions the [practice guide](/practice-guides/asset-purchase/us) covers. Optional means the parties may choose the provision. Recommended safeguards under an optional provision apply only if it is selected; they do not recommend selecting it. An unchecked review box is not a deal election.

This checklist states drafting expectations, not a guarantee of enforceability. The [Online Business Asset Purchase Agreement](/templates/openagreements-online-business-asset-purchase-agreement) creates a binding agreement before a scheduled Closing and requires express interim-period, condition, delivery and termination mechanics.

## Scope and allocation {#scope-and-allocation}

- [ ] **Identify parties** (Recommended) — Match every payment, conveyance and signature obligation to a named party.
  The agreement SHOULD identify each purchaser and seller by legal name and signing capacity. [^p3-greenfield-joinder] [^p3-meta-lodge-joinder]
  Entity/individual capacity and additional asset owners remain fact-dependent.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-structure-and-parties/us#owner-signing-capacity). [#identify-parties]

- [ ] **Confirm asset sale scope** (Recommended) — Trace the operative grant to assets; flag stock, membership interests or alternative transaction branches.
  The agreement SHOULD identify the transaction as a transfer of specified assets rather than a transfer of ownership interests in the seller. [^d1-mellor-owner-shares] [^p2-grove-acquired-assets]
  Equity or mixed transactions require separately scoped review.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-what-transfers/us#asset-and-liability-perimeter). [#confirm-asset-sale-scope]

- [ ] **Identify purchased assets** (Recommended) — For each asset class, locate the grant and a way to identify its members.
  The agreement SHOULD identify the purchased assets through a schedule or other objectively ascertainable description. [^ratschko-clear-asset-inclusions-exclusions] [^p2-grove-acquired-schedule]
  The grant may enumerate assets, convey a bounded business-wide category, or both.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-what-transfers/us#asset-and-liability-perimeter). [#identify-purchased-assets]

- [ ] **Identify excluded assets** (Recommended) — Trace every exclusion against the grant; an express none election is assessable.
  The agreement SHOULD identify assets retained by the seller and resolve conflicts with the purchased-asset description. [^ratschko-clear-asset-inclusions-exclusions] [^p2-grove-excluded-assets]
  Cash, receivables, names and shared tools are choices, not automatic exclusions.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-what-transfers/us#asset-and-liability-perimeter). [#identify-excluded-assets]

- [ ] **Identify assumed liabilities** (Recommended) — Match each assumed category to an obligation, period and responsible party.
  The agreement SHOULD identify the obligations the purchaser expressly assumes. [^hodson-contractual-liability-allocation] [^p2-grove-assumed-liabilities]
  Categories are limited or broadened deliberately; third-party exposure requires separate review. An asset-sale structure does not by itself rule out successor liability, a theory that has expanded over time. [^p1-kddk-asset-buyer-exceptions]
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-what-transfers/us#asset-and-liability-perimeter). [#identify-assumed-liabilities]

- [ ] **Identify retained liabilities** (Recommended) — Read the retained-liability rule together with every assumption and exception.
  The agreement SHOULD allocate liabilities not expressly assumed to the seller as between the parties. [^hodson-contractual-liability-allocation] [^p2-grove-retained-liabilities]
  This criterion evaluates the allocation between the contracting parties; third-party exposure requires separate review. A seller's creditors are not parties to the agreement and are not bound by it; a bankruptcy-court or other judicial sale may be an exception. [^d1-hodson-creditors-not-bound]
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-what-transfers/us#asset-and-liability-perimeter). [#identify-retained-liabilities]

- [ ] **Set performance cutoff** (Recommended) — Locate date, time and treatment of transactions spanning the cutoff.
  The agreement SHOULD state the effective cutoff for allocating business receipts, expenses and performance between the parties. [^d2-salem-receivables-cutoff] [^d2-sitestar-prorations]
  Closing time and economic effective time may differ if expressly reconciled.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-after-closing/us#customer-obligation-cutoff). [#set-performance-cutoff]

## Digital assets and rights {#digital-assets-and-rights}

- [ ] **Establish ip chain of title** (Recommended) — Only if intellectual property is included, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Trace material IP to creation or acquisition records and identify missing assignments.
  The agreement SHOULD require disclosure of the basis on which the seller owns the intellectual property being transferred. [^p4-luna-ip-ownership] [^salem-owned-business-ip] [^bankrate-seller-owned-ip]
  Qualifiers and remediation for missing records remain negotiable.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-what-transfers/us#ip-title-and-dependencies). [#establish-ip-chain-of-title]

- [ ] **Effect ip transfer** (Recommended) — Only if intellectual property is included, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Locate the conveyance and identify any separate instrument needed to complete it.
  The agreement SHOULD provide an operative transfer mechanism for the included intellectual property. [^d1-greenfield-copyright-assignment] [^p3-grove-ip-assignments]
  Instrument form and formalities require asset- and jurisdiction-specific review.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-what-transfers/us#copyright-transfer-formality). [#effect-ip-transfer]

- [ ] **Inventory third party licenses** (Recommended) — Only if third-party dependencies exist, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Locate license/provider, covered component and restriction information for each material dependency.
  The agreement SHOULD identify third-party rights needed to use the transferred business assets. [^ip-contracts-ip-title-and-dependencies] [^salem-owned-business-ip] [^bankrate-seller-owned-ip]
  Open-source, commercial, stock-media and hosted-service dependencies merit separate entries.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-what-transfers/us#ip-title-and-dependencies). [#inventory-third-party-licenses]

- [ ] **Address shared retained ip** (Recommended) — Only if operation depends on retained intellectual property, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Locate permitted use, duration and termination terms for the retained dependency.
  The agreement SHOULD specify the purchaser's post-closing use rights in retained seller intellectual property needed by the business. [^p4-salem-shared-code-license] [^ecotality-interim-domain-use]
  License, replacement or elimination of the dependency are alternatives.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-what-transfers/us#ip-title-and-dependencies). [#address-shared-retained-ip]

## Platforms and contracts {#platforms-and-contracts}

- [ ] **Identify assigned contracts** (Recommended) — Only if customer or vendor contracts are involved, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Match the schedule to customer subscriptions, suppliers and operational services.
  The agreement SHOULD identify the customer and vendor contracts to be assigned. [^sitestar-vendor-contracts-schedule] [^ecotality-assumed-contracts-definition] [^settles-anti-assignment]
  The schedule covers relevant amendments and orders and is not presumed to include every business contract.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-what-transfers/us#asset-and-liability-perimeter). [#identify-assigned-contracts]

- [ ] **Track required consents** (Recommended) — Only if customer or vendor contracts are involved or platform accounts are involved, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Trace each consent dependency to an owner and evidence of approval or unresolved status.
  The agreement SHOULD identify required third-party consents and who is responsible for obtaining them. [^p3-luna-consents-rep] [^p3-greenfield-consents-rep] [^p3-luna-seller-best-efforts]
  Consent effort standard, expense allocation and deadlines are negotiated.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-closing-and-handover/us#consent-failure). [#track-required-consents]

- [ ] **Resolve unavailable transfers** (Recommended) — Only if customer or vendor contracts are involved or platform accounts are involved, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Locate a consequence tied to the affected asset or contract, not only a general cooperation promise.
  The agreement SHOULD specify the consequence if a material transfer or consent remains unavailable at closing. [^p3-salem-pass-through] [^settles-consent-escrow]
  Delay, exclusion, adjustment, termination or a permitted interim arrangement are alternatives; no default selected.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-closing-and-handover/us#consent-failure). [#resolve-unavailable-transfers]

## Price and elections {#price-and-elections}

- [ ] **State consideration** (Recommended) — Reconcile the stated price with cash, noncash, deferred and contingent components.
  The agreement SHOULD state the consideration and when and how each component is payable. [^softech-closing-price-holdback] [^ivfh-post-closing-payment] [^ratschko-promissory-note]
  No financing mix or payment split is presumed.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-price-and-payment/us#escrow-holdback-and-deferred-payment). [#state-consideration]

- [ ] **Elect price adjustment** (Optional) — Identify an affirmative election; omission is not a defect for this optional feature.
  The agreement MAY include a post-closing purchase-price adjustment. [^d2-schwabe-adjustment-purpose]
  Fixed price and adjustment are legitimate alternatives.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-price-and-payment/us#closing-price-adjustment). [#elect-price-adjustment]

- [ ] **Define adjustment calculation** (Recommended) — Only if a price adjustment is elected, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Recalculate a worked example using the stated inputs and prevent double counting.
  The agreement SHOULD define the adjustment formula and accounting conventions. [^d2-schwabe-accounting-principles] [^p5-luna-net-working-capital]
  Working capital, inventory, cash or debt measures are not universal requirements.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-price-and-payment/us#closing-price-adjustment). [#define-adjustment-calculation]

- [ ] **Resolve adjustment disputes** (Recommended) — Only if a price adjustment is elected, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Locate delivery deadline, access to records, objection process and decision endpoint.
  The agreement SHOULD specify how adjustment statements are reviewed and disputed. [^barlow-specific-procedures] [^barlow-review-period]
  Expert determination and litigation/arbitration routes require deliberate coordination.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-price-and-payment/us#closing-price-adjustment). [#resolve-adjustment-disputes]

- [ ] **Earnout** (Optional) — Identify the contingent-price election; absence is not a failure.
  The agreement MAY make part of the price contingent on post-closing performance. [^d2-pm-schwabe-earnout]
  No earnout amount, period or metric is preselected.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-price-and-payment/us#price-mechanisms). [#elect-earnout]

- [ ] **Define earnout measure** (Recommended) — Only if an earnout is elected, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Compute a synthetic period from the definition, including inclusions and exclusions.
  The agreement SHOULD define the earnout metric, measurement period and calculation method. [^d2-dwt-define-accounting]
  Caps, floors and acceleration are choices.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-price-and-payment/us#price-mechanisms). [#define-earnout-measure]

- [ ] **Define earnout operating covenant** (Recommended) — Only if an earnout is elected, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Locate express commitments or an express no-commitment position and evaluate metric interaction.
  The agreement SHOULD state the operating commitments, if any, that apply during the earnout period. [^dwt-earnout-ordinary-course]
  This criterion does not call for an express duty to maximize the earnout.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-price-and-payment/us#price-mechanisms). [#define-earnout-operating-covenant]

- [ ] **Provide earnout review** (Recommended) — Only if an earnout is elected, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Locate reporting, supporting-record access, objection deadline and resolution mechanism.
  The agreement SHOULD specify the seller's process for reviewing and disputing earnout calculations. [^d2-dwt-four-areas] [^earnout-review-price-mechanisms]
  Access, confidentiality and independent review are tailored to the deal.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-price-and-payment/us#price-mechanisms). [#provide-earnout-review]

- [ ] **Elect escrow or holdback** (Optional) — Identify whether either mechanism is elected; absence is not a failure.
  The agreement MAY reserve part of the price in escrow or as a holdback. [^d2-dbl-escrow-defined] [^softech-closing-price-holdback]
  Escrow and buyer-held amounts have different custody risks.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-price-and-payment/us#escrow-holdback-and-deferred-payment). [#elect-escrow-or-holdback]

- [ ] **Define reserve release** (Recommended) — Only if a purchase-price escrow or holdback is elected, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Trace an undisputed release and a disputed claim through the mechanism.
  The agreement SHOULD define the amount reserved and the conditions and timing for its release. [^d2-schwabe-holdback-amount] [^schwabe-holdback-survival] [^dbl-graduated-release]
  Custodian, interest, fees and permitted deductions remain negotiated.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-price-and-payment/us#escrow-holdback-and-deferred-payment). [#define-reserve-release]

## Representations and interim conduct {#representations-and-interim-conduct}

- [ ] **Allocate transfer taxes** (Recommended) — Identify the taxpayer-cost allocation and any contractual reimbursement duty.
  The agreement SHOULD specify which party bears transaction taxes. [^d2-greenfield-transfer-taxes] [^grove-transfer-taxes-shared]
  Jurisdiction and tax treatment need transaction-specific review; no default chosen.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-price-and-payment/us#purchase-price-allocation). [#allocate-transfer-taxes]

- [ ] **Coordinate price allocation reporting** (Recommended) — Identify the allocation preparation, review, timing and disagreement process.
  The agreement SHOULD specify a process for coordinating purchase-price allocation reporting. [^d2-sitestar-consistent-reporting] [^p5-amerbrew-allocation-schedule]
  Jurisdiction and tax treatment need transaction-specific review; no default chosen.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-price-and-payment/us#purchase-price-allocation). [#coordinate-price-allocation-reporting]

- [ ] **Represent authority** (Recommended) — Check party capacity and approvals against the promised obligations.
  The agreement SHOULD include each party's statement of authority to enter and perform the transaction. [^igourmet-seller-authority] [^bankrate-owner-authority]
  Scope differs for entities and individuals.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-structure-and-parties/us#owner-signing-capacity). [#represent-authority]

- [ ] **Represent title and liens** (Recommended) — Locate title statement, disclosed encumbrances and any release commitments.
  The agreement SHOULD state the seller's ownership and lien position for the purchased assets. [^d1-salem-good-title] [^salinas-payoff-condition]
  Permitted encumbrances and lien-release conditions are negotiated.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-what-transfers/us#liens-and-title). [#represent-title-and-liens]

- [ ] **Disclose material business disputes** (Recommended) — Locate dispute scope and disclosure record; distinguish actual knowledge from absolute assurance.
  The agreement SHOULD require disclosure of material pending or threatened disputes affecting the transferred business. [^salem-business-litigation-rep] [^greenfield-litigation-rep]
  Knowledge, materiality and lookback are choices.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-what-transfers/us#schedules). [#disclose-material-business-disputes]

- [ ] **Coordinate disclosure schedules** (Recommended) — Trace each exception to the representation it qualifies.
  The agreement SHOULD link exceptions to representations to identifiable disclosure-schedule entries. [^conway-lists-exceptions] [^meyers-scheduled-exception] [^p2-luna-consent-schedule]
  If cross-disclosure is used, its effect is stated.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-what-transfers/us#schedules). [#coordinate-disclosure-schedules]

- [ ] **Preserve business before closing** (Recommended) — Only if signing precedes closing, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Locate commitments addressing material changes to the business being acquired.
  The agreement SHOULD specify the seller's operating commitments between signing and closing. [^p3-greenfield-conduct] [^p3-bankrate-conduct]
  Ordinary-course standard, exceptions and purchaser-consent process require negotiation.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-signing-to-closing/us#between-signing-and-closing). [#preserve-business-before-closing]

## Closing and transition {#closing-and-transition}

- [ ] **Define closing mechanics** (Recommended) — Trace payment and conveyance sequencing to a defined closing event.
  The agreement SHOULD state the closing process and the event at which the parties exchange performance. [^p3-grove-simultaneous] [^p3-luna-closing-payments]
  Simultaneous exchange, escrow closing and transfers in stages are alternatives.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-closing-and-handover/us#closing-exchange). [#define-closing-mechanics]

- [ ] **Specify closing conditions** (Recommended) — Only if signing precedes closing, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Match each condition to the entitled party, satisfaction evidence and waiver process.
  The agreement SHOULD identify the conditions to each party's obligation to close. [^p3-greenfield-deliveries-condition] [^consents-closing-exchange]
  Financing, material adverse change and diligence conditions are not automatic.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-closing-and-handover/us#closing-exchange). [#specify-closing-conditions]

- [ ] **Complete closing deliverables** (Recommended) — Reconcile the deliverable list with transfer instruments, approvals, releases and payment documents actually required by the deal.
  The agreement SHOULD identify each closing deliverable and the party responsible for providing it. [^p3-luna-bill-of-sale] [^p3-grove-ip-domain] [^p3-chefs-flow-of-funds]
  A deliverable is conditional on the corresponding asset, liability or election.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-closing-and-handover/us#closing-exchange). [#complete-closing-deliverables]

- [ ] **Elect transition support** (Optional) — Identify the election; no support obligation is inferred from silence.
  The agreement MAY require the seller to provide post-closing transition services. [^venn-transition-term]
  No fixed hours, term or fee is presumed.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-after-closing/us#transition-and-completion). [#elect-transition-support]

- [ ] **Define transition service scope** (Recommended) — Only if transition support is elected, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Locate deliverables, service boundaries, end date and payment rule.
  The agreement SHOULD specify the tasks, duration and compensation for transition services. [^d3-venn-structure] [^venn-transition-pay]
  Named personnel appear only where agreed and available.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-after-closing/us#transition-and-completion). [#define-transition-service-scope]

- [ ] **Provide further assurances** (Recommended) — Locate limits, expense responsibility and an endpoint or objective completion standard.
  The agreement SHOULD specify reasonable post-closing cooperation to complete agreed transfers. [^d3-bankrate-further-assurances] [^p3-grove-further-assurances]
  This does not substitute for required third-party permission.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-after-closing/us#transition-and-completion). [#provide-further-assurances]

## Remedies and general terms {#remedies-and-general-terms}

- [ ] **Define preclosing termination** (Recommended) — Only if signing precedes closing, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Trace outside date, breach and failed-condition scenarios to an eligible party and consequence.
  The agreement SHOULD identify termination rights and their consequences before closing. [^p3-luna-outside-date] [^d3-bankrate-effect-of-termination] [^p5-ecotality-termination-grounds]
  Cure periods, deposits and surviving duties are choices.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-signing-to-closing/us#between-signing-and-closing). [#define-preclosing-termination]

- [ ] **Elect indemnification** (Optional) — Identify election and covered subjects; absence is not a failure of this optional mechanism.
  The agreement MAY provide contractual indemnification for identified losses. [^d2-crosier-leverage] [^luna-indemnity-basket]
  Other contractual remedies require separate review; no universal indemnity is prescribed.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-after-closing/us#remedy-allocation). [#elect-indemnification]

- [ ] **Define indemnity allocation** (Recommended) — Only if indemnification is elected, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Trace breach, assumed-liability and retained-liability examples to the correct indemnitor.
  The agreement SHOULD identify the indemnifying party, protected party and covered claim categories. [^jeffs-indemnity-pre-migration] [^p3-greenfield-shareholder-indemnity]
  Covered categories and exclusions remain negotiated.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-after-closing/us#remedy-allocation). [#define-indemnity-allocation]

- [ ] **Define indemnity claims process** (Recommended) — Only if indemnification is elected, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Locate notice, defense control, settlement consent and payment mechanics.
  The agreement SHOULD specify the procedure for direct and third-party indemnity claims. [^d3-luna-claim-response] [^salem-third-party-claim-notice]
  The effect of late notice is defined rather than assumed to be automatic forfeiture.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-after-closing/us#remedy-allocation). [#define-indemnity-claims-process]

- [ ] **Address claim survival** (Recommended) — Trace a timely and a late claim separately for each claim category.
  The agreement SHOULD state any agreed survival periods or contractual claim deadlines. [^luna-micron-representation-survival] [^igourmet-apa-representation-survival]
  An express choice to impose no contractual deadline is assessable; counsel reviews legal limits.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-after-closing/us#remedy-allocation). [#address-claim-survival]

- [ ] **Address liability caps** (Recommended) — Apply a claim above the stated cap and identify category-specific exceptions.
  The agreement SHOULD state whether contractual recovery is capped and identify any selected cap. [^crosier-small-deal-cap] [^luna-indemnity-cap] [^igourmet-apa-indemnity-cap]
  No monetary limit, percentage or uncapped category is preselected.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-after-closing/us#remedy-allocation). [#address-liability-caps]

- [ ] **Address indemnity thresholds** (Recommended) — Only if indemnification is elected, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Distinguish deductible and tipping mechanics using a synthetic claim.
  The agreement SHOULD state whether indemnity recovery is subject to a threshold and how any selected threshold operates. [^d2-warf-tipping-defined] [^d2-warf-deductible-defined] [^igourmet-apa-indemnity-deductible]
  No basket or de minimis amount is prescribed.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-after-closing/us#remedy-allocation). [#address-indemnity-thresholds]

- [ ] **Address remedy exclusivity** (Recommended) — Trace a contractual breach to the remedies preserved or excluded.
  The agreement SHOULD state whether an agreed remedy is exclusive and identify any exceptions. [^grove-exclusive-indemnity-remedy] [^salem-exclusive-remedy-fraud-exception]
  Specific performance and equitable relief remain separate review questions.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-after-closing/us#remedy-allocation). [#address-remedy-exclusivity]

- [ ] **Address fraud treatment** (Recommended) — Identify relevant actors, statements and limitation exceptions without assuming enforceability.
  The agreement SHOULD state the intended treatment of fraud in the agreed remedy limitations. [^salem-exclusive-remedy-fraud-exception] [^igourmet-apa-indemnity-cap]
  Counsel must assess the definition and enforceability under applicable law; no legal safe harbor is asserted.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-after-closing/us#remedy-allocation). [#address-fraud-treatment]

- [ ] **Address setoff** (Recommended) — Only if price payments are deferred or an earnout is elected, review the following safeguard. Otherwise mark it not applicable.
  Trace a disputed claim against an installment or earnout payment.
  The agreement SHOULD state whether claims may be set off against unpaid consideration. [^d2-grove-setoff] [^d2-salem-setoff] [^dbl-setoff-weaker]
  Consent, claim determination and dispute-reserve rules are choices.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-price-and-payment/us#escrow-holdback-and-deferred-payment). [#address-setoff]

- [ ] **Elect restrictive covenants** (Optional) — Identify election without treating covenant presence as evidence of enforceability.
  The agreement MAY include a separately reviewed sale-related restrictive covenant. [^d3-luna-nonsolicitation]
  Scope, duration, territory and permitted activities require jurisdiction-specific analysis; no employment rule is imported.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-after-closing/us#seller-covenants). [#elect-restrictive-covenants]

- [ ] **Protect transaction confidentiality** (Recommended) — Locate permitted disclosures and survival treatment or identify a controlling separate agreement.
  The agreement SHOULD allocate confidentiality and permitted announcement responsibilities for transaction information. [^p5-igourmet-announcements] [^p5-igourmet-confidentiality-terms] [^p5-luna-announcement-consult]
  Disclosures to advisers, providers and customers are coordinated.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-signing-to-closing/us#general-terms). [#protect-transaction-confidentiality]

- [ ] **Select governing law** (Recommended) — Locate a definite governing-law selection.
  The agreement SHOULD identify the chosen governing law. [^p5-igourmet-law-and-forum] [^greenfield-delaware-governing-law]
  Counsel reviews conflicts and mandatory rules separately.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-signing-to-closing/us#general-terms). [#select-governing-law]

- [ ] **Select dispute process** (Recommended) — Reconcile general disputes with adjustment, earnout and urgent-relief mechanisms.
  The agreement SHOULD specify the process and forum for resolving contractual disputes. [^p5-igourmet-law-and-forum] [^p5-sitestar-arbitration]
  Court and arbitration are alternatives, not a ranked default.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-signing-to-closing/us#general-terms). [#select-dispute-process]

- [ ] **Define notices** (Recommended) — Test a claim or termination notice against recipient, method and receipt rule.
  The agreement SHOULD specify how contractual notices are delivered and when they become effective. [^p5-luna-notices] [^salem-notice-deemed-given]
  Business email and formal notice addresses may differ.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-signing-to-closing/us#general-terms). [#define-notices]

- [ ] **Resolve document priority** (Recommended) — Trace an inconsistency to a stated controlling instrument or resolution rule.
  The agreement SHOULD resolve priority among the agreement, schedules and ancillary transaction documents. [^d3-sitestar-agreement-controls] [^p5-igourmet-conflicts]
  Entire-agreement and amendment provisions fit the actual document package.
  [Drafting context](/practice-guides/selling-an-app-business/us#marketplace-process). [#resolve-document-priority]

[^p1-kddk-asset-buyer-exceptions]: **Successor liability in the purchase of a business, part 1 (Kahn, Dees, Donovan & Kahn), exceptions** — "The law in most jurisdictions has traditionally held that when one company sells all of its assets to another, the buyer does not become liable for the debts and liabilities of the selling company. This is still true to a large extent, and we frequently recommend structuring a transaction as an asset purchase in order to protect the buyer from the liabilities of the business being acquired. However, over the past several years, the theory of successor liability has evolved and expanded as a result of a series of clashes between the policy in favor of allowing a company to sell its assets in an unrestricted manner, balanced against other policies, such as providing a source of relief for injured parties or other claimants. As a result, in some cases, a purchaser of assets may be held liable for certain liabilities of the seller." *Jeffrey K. Helfrich, Successor Liability in the Purchase of a Business: Express or Implied Assumption of Obligations (Part 1 of 8), Kahn, Dees, Donovan & Kahn, LLP (May 5, 2015).* <https://kddk.com/2015/05/05/successor-liability-in-the-purchase-of-a-business-part-1-of-8/>

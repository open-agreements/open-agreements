# Selling an App Business in the United States[^about]

How the owner of an app or small online software business sells it, often through a marketplace and an escrow service: preparing the listing and its figures, diligence access to the code, shared accounts and keys, the purchase agreement, handing over each asset, the period after closing and limits on the seller's exposure.

## How does selling an app business through an online marketplace work, from listing to escrow release? {#marketplace-sale-steps}

**Short answer.** When a marketplace sale runs through Escrow.com with a broker as a party, the buyer and seller sign their own purchase agreement and Escrow.com holds the buyer’s money while the seller transfers the app, releasing it when the buyer accepts or the inspection period ends. [^flippa-contract-between-parties] [^escrow-broker-sale-sequence]

Flippa’s listing terms require complete and accurate information, including current and historical financial data, and make the seller responsible for keeping the listing current. [^flippa-listing-accurate-data] Acquire.com makes the seller solely responsible for its listing materials, including financial information. [^acquire-seller-materials]

Flippa and Acquire.com state that they are not parties to the buyer’s and seller’s purchase agreement. [^flippa-contract-between-parties] [^acquire-not-party] Flippa also states that it is not an escrow service or payment provider and that independent providers handle payment processing. [^flippa-not-escrow]

Escrow.com’s definition of property eligible for escrow includes software, source code, domain names and intellectual property. [^escrow-merchandise-includes-code] Its instructions for a sale involving a broker call for transfer after funds are secured and verified, inspection after confirmed receipt, and payment after acceptance or inspection expiry. [^escrow-broker-sale-sequence]

Acquire.com leaves closing items, including tax or lien clearances, bulk-sale notices and purchase-price allocation forms, to the parties. [^acquire-closing-items]

The later questions on this page follow this order, with the buyer’s counterpart in [Buying an App Business](/practice-guides/buying-an-app-business/us).

## Can the buyer and seller use their own purchase agreement or an addendum when a marketplace runs the sale? {#marketplace-process}

**Short answer.** A separate purchase agreement or addendum in a marketplace sale can be constrained by the marketplace's terms, such as TrustMRR's current terms requiring deals initiated there to be completed through its platform and requiring a new revision and fresh signatures for any change after the first electronic signature. [^trustmrr-complete-through-platform][^marketplace-revision-marketplace-process]

Marketplace terms can distinguish the platform's completion process, the acquisition documents and a separate escrow service, and TrustMRR's terms describe an approval route for using an alternative process: they require contacting its support and obtaining its written approval before completing a transaction outside its standard escrow. [^d3-trustmrr-approval-route] An alternative process that requires the platform's prior approval is not permission to bypass the platform, and TrustMRR's terms prohibit circumventing it on pain of account suspension or termination. [^d3-trustmrr-no-circumvention] Nor does such an approval establish that the escrow agent will accept a separate document, because TrustMRR's terms treat Escrow.com as a separate, independent service governed by its own terms. [^p6-trustmrr-escrow-separate]

An addendum also has to satisfy the purchase agreement's own amendment clause; one filed agreement, for example, permitted amendment only by a written agreement executed by the party to be charged. [^amendment-document-priority] A clear addendum can identify the underlying agreement by parties, date and signed revision, name the changed sections, state which unchanged terms continue and be signed as the amendment clause requires; one marketplace-brokered agreement permitted amendment in writing only if both parties signed and dated it. [^d3-jeffs-amendment] An express priority clause resolves a conflict that document dates alone may leave unanswered, such as a later escrow instruction or a marketplace's standard form that covers the same subject; one filed agreement provided that its own provisions control over any other document in a conflict. [^d3-sitestar-agreement-controls]

TrustMRR's FAQ says it uploads the signed APA to the Escrow.com transaction. [^p6-trustmrr-signed-apa-upload] An addendum that is outside the signed revision, or that the escrow agent has not accepted, may therefore not control the release of funds, and TrustMRR's terms send disputes about escrow funds and disbursements to Escrow.com directly. [^d3-trustmrr-escrow-disputes] An email that only discusses a change, without the agreement and signature the clause requires, is not an executed amendment under a clause that, like the one above, requires a written agreement executed by the party to be charged. [^amendment-document-priority]

Whether the standard form needs changing at all is covered under [the marketplace's standard agreement](/practice-guides/buying-an-app-business/us#marketplace-standard-form).

## Can a seller change app pricing or free trials before listing the app for sale? {#pricing-changes-before-listing}

**Short answer.** Flippa’s quoted listing terms address accuracy rather than pricing changes, requiring the listing’s current and historical financial data to be complete, accurate and kept up to date. [^flippa-listing-accurate-data-before-listing]

Flippa requires current and historical financial data and makes the seller responsible for keeping the listing up to date. [^flippa-listing-accurate-data-before-listing] Its users also warrant that posted content is accurate when posted. [^flippa-content-accurate-warranty]

Apple’s financial reports show monthly proceeds and final unit sales on its fiscal calendar. [^apple-financial-reports-monthly] Apple defines proceeds per unit as the customer price minus applicable taxes and its commission. [^apple-partner-share-net]

Contract promises can reach both the figures already supplied and conduct after signing: one filed agreement facilitated by a marketplace broker required truthful and accurate details about revenue, profit and expenses. [^jeffs-seller-truthful-details] That agreement also required the seller to maintain the business as it had operated before the sale and avoid actions outside normal business practices during migration. [^jeffs-ordinary-course] Under that wording, a pricing experiment outside normal business practices during migration would conflict with the seller’s promise, and a price change made before listing but left out of the revenue details would sit uneasily with the accuracy warranty. [^jeffs-ordinary-course] [^jeffs-seller-truthful-details]

## Can a seller stand behind the revenue and retention figures its own dashboard shows buyers? {#dashboard-figures-accuracy}

**Short answer.** How far a seller stands behind a dashboard figure depends on the wording of its contractual promise, and marketplace terms already make the seller responsible for the financial information in its listing. [^jeffs-seller-truthful-details-figures-accuracy] [^acquire-seller-materials-figures-accuracy] [^flippa-listing-accurate-data-figures-accuracy]

Acquire.com assigns responsibility for listing materials and financial information to the seller, while Flippa requires complete, accurate and current listing information. [^acquire-seller-materials-figures-accuracy] [^flippa-listing-accurate-data-figures-accuracy]

Google Play says its estimated sales report is not recommended for accounting and can differ from earnings because it does not take into account withholding taxes or chargebacks. [^play-estimated-not-accounting] Its monthly earnings report shows payouts and transactions. [^play-earnings-monthly] Apple’s financial reports show monthly proceeds, with proceeds per unit calculated after applicable taxes and Apple’s commission. [^apple-financial-reports-monthly-figures-accuracy] [^apple-partner-share-net-figures-accuracy]

One filed marketplace-brokered agreement qualified its litigation warranty by the seller’s knowledge but gave an unqualified warranty that revenue, profit and expense details supplied to the broker were truthful and accurate. [^jeffs-seller-truthful-details-figures-accuracy]

Flippa’s "Data Verified"[^flippa-verified-not-diligence] and "Vetted by Flippa"[^flippa-verified-not-diligence] labels carry no warranty of data accuracy or completeness and do not replace the buyer’s review, which is described in [checking subscription revenue against app-store reports](/practice-guides/buying-an-app-business/us#subscription-revenue-app-store-reports). [^flippa-verified-not-diligence]

## Will an app built with an AI app builder keep working after it is handed over to a buyer? {#app-runs-after-handover}

**Short answer.** The buyer can maintain and update the app only if it also receives the code and build materials, because an App Store transfer moves the listing while Apple leaves delivery of the code and build materials to the seller. [^apple-transferor-delivers-code]

Apple states that the app can remain available during transfer, retaining its reviews, ratings and bundle ID while users continue receiving updates. [^apple-transfer-keeps-reviews] The buyer must create new provisioning profiles (the signing set-up for the app) in its own developer account after transfer. [^apple-provisioning-profiles]

Connected services have their own requirements: Vercel says project integrations must be added again after transfer. [^vercel-integrations-again] Google Play requires updates to account settings and apps for integrated services such as Firebase and Google Analytics. [^play-integrated-services]

For a website moving between hosts, Google recommends uploading a copy to the new host and thoroughly testing every way users interact with it. [^google-copy-and-test] A seller that has run that kind of test outside the AI app builder can describe the result, and one that has not can say so, because one filed marketplace-brokered agreement had the seller warrant that the details it supplied to the broker about the asset were truthful and accurate. [^jeffs-seller-truthful-details-after-handover]

## Which account pays for each service the app still uses, and what can stop working when accounts move? {#who-pays-for-each-service}

**Short answer.** A paid service can keep billing the account it sits in until it moves, and moving an account out of its organization can stop a service integrated there, as Amazon Web Services’ rules for an account leaving an organization show. [^aws-new-costs-owner] [^aws-integration-stops]

Receiving accounts may need payment arrangements before transfer: Vercel requires a valid payment method on the receiving team to avoid interrupted service. [^vercel-payment-method] Google Play requires an active payments profile in the receiving account for paid apps and apps with in-app products. [^play-payments-profile]

Amazon Web Services makes an account’s owner responsible for all new costs from the moment the account leaves an organization, using that account’s payment method. [^aws-new-costs-owner]

Service connections need separate attention: removing an account from an organization with an enabled Amazon Web Services integration prevents that account’s users from using the integrated service. [^aws-integration-stops] Vercel requires integrations associated with a transferred project to be added again after transfer. [^vercel-integrations-again-each-service]

For a payment account sold with the business, Stripe requires the existing owner to contact Stripe Support first to confirm which information needs updating. [^stripe-business-sale-contact]

## How can a seller let a buyer review the code during diligence without handing it over? {#code-access-during-diligence}

**Short answer.** A seller can give a prospective buyer GitHub’s Read role on a private organization repository and remove that access if the deal fails, although removal does not erase copies the buyer already downloaded. [^github-read-role] [^github-removed-keeps-clones]

GitHub describes Read as its least-access organization repository role, intended for people who want to view or discuss a project. [^github-read-role]

Removal has limits: GitHub states that removed collaborators retain local copies and that the repository owner is responsible for ensuring that people who lose access delete confidential information. [^github-removed-keeps-clones] Acquire.com’s confidentiality terms require recipients to use evaluation materials only to assess a possible transaction and keep them confidential, with limited disclosure to representatives who need them for that purpose. [^acquire-evaluation-material]

A shared access key can outlast account removal: GitHub warns that anyone holding the private part of a deploy key retains its permitted read or write access even after removal from the organization. [^github-deploy-key-warning]

Review access also differs from final delivery: Apple makes the transferor responsible for exchanging the actual code and build materials and explaining the app’s capabilities and App Store configuration. [^apple-transferor-delivers-code-during-diligence]

## If a seller cleaned up the code for the sale, does it need to tell the buyer the live app is different? {#cleaned-up-code-disclosure}

**Short answer.** A seller that warrants the accuracy of the details it supplied for the sale is safer telling the buyer that the delivered code differs from the version users run, because the store listing and the code transfer separately. [^jeffs-seller-truthful-details-code-disclosure] [^apple-transferor-delivers-code-code-disclosure]

Apple allows the live app to remain available during transfer with its reviews, ratings and bundle ID. [^apple-transfer-keeps-reviews-code-disclosure] The seller separately exchanges the actual code and build materials and informs the buyer about capabilities and App Store configuration. [^apple-transferor-delivers-code-code-disclosure] Apple also directs the buyer to review the previous owner’s privacy disclosures when accepting the transfer, which is one reason buyers [review the live app as well as the repository](/practice-guides/buying-an-app-business/us#review-live-app-or-repository). [^apple-accept-privacy-details]

One filed marketplace-brokered agreement required the seller to maintain the asset as it had operated before sale and avoid actions outside normal business practices during migration. [^jeffs-ordinary-course-code-disclosure]

In *ABRY Partners V, L.P. v. F & W Acquisition LLC*, the Delaware Court of Chancery held that a contractual cap cannot restrict the buyer’s remedy for a seller’s intentional misrepresentation of a fact stated in the contract, which matters when the agreement describes the code being delivered. [^abry-lies-not-capped]

## Does a seller have to give up keys or accounts that its other apps also use? {#keys-shared-with-other-apps}

**Short answer.** A seller can keep a key or account that its other apps also use if the agreement excludes it from the sale, and Apple lets a seller remove a Sign in with Apple Service ID’s association before transfer so that the Service ID stays behind. [^salem-shared-code-excluded] [^apple-sign-in-grouped]

One filed agreement, for example, excluded code, design and functionality shared with the seller’s other websites, so their ownership stayed with the seller. [^salem-shared-code-excluded]

Platform connections can still affect retained apps: Apple states that other apps lose access to a shared CloudKit data container when it transfers with an app. [^apple-cloudkit-shared] Apps grouped for Sign in with Apple must be ungrouped before transfer, and the associated service identifier transfers unless its association is removed first. [^apple-sign-in-grouped]

For automatically renewing subscriptions, Apple requires the seller to generate and share an app-specific shared secret before starting transfer. [^apple-shared-secret-initiator] The buyer obtains that secret before acceptance and generates a new one after transfer to end outside access. [^apple-shared-secret]

GitHub warns that possession of a deploy key’s private key preserves its permitted access even after the holder leaves the organization. [^github-deploy-key-warning-other-apps]

## How can the seller separate a shared account from its other businesses, and how is the separation documented? {#shared-accounts}

**Short answer.** A seller can separate a shared account by moving the sold business's project or sub-account out of its wider account through the provider's own transfer route, as AWS allows when a member account is removed from an organization and becomes a standalone account rather than being closed. [^p6-aws-remove-standalone]

Google Cloud expressly anticipates this kind of separation; its documentation says projects may need to move between organizations because of acquisitions or the separation of business units. [^p6-gcp-separation] The route depends on how the sold business sits inside the seller's account, and a business that already has its own project can move as a unit; Google Cloud describes a project migration as something other than a data transfer, during which the project's services, databases and virtual machines stay active without downtime. [^d3-gcp-not-data-transfer] Resources mixed into a project that also serves the seller's other businesses can be recreated in a new buyer account and their data migrated, or served by the seller for a limited time under the [transition terms](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-after-closing/us#transition-and-completion); one filed online-service agreement, for example, capped the seller's transition assistance with customer accounts and billing at 90 days. [^d3-sitestar-transition-cap]

A separation can leave access behind; AWS states that an access role created for the organization's management account is not deleted automatically when a member account leaves the organization, [^p6-aws-iam-role-remains] so a separation checklist can cover users, roles, API keys, billing, data, integrations and recovery methods on both sides.

The separation record can list what moved, what stayed with the seller and why, and the date on which control changed, and that date can also move the charges; AWS makes the departing account's owner responsible for all new costs from the moment the account leaves the organization. [^p6-aws-billing-moves] Ownership, billing and contact details are separate completion items, and AWS requires an account becoming standalone to have a support plan, verified contact information and a current payment method. [^d3-aws-standalone-requirements] An acceptance check tests the resulting service, because separation can change what the account keeps; AWS states that a departing account loses access to cost and usage data from its time in the organization [^d3-aws-cost-data] and that users in the account can no longer use an AWS service whose integration was enabled for the organization. [^d3-aws-integration-lost]

## What should a seller check when the buyer wants a full asset purchase agreement on top of the marketplace's form? {#full-apa-on-top-of-marketplace-form}

**Short answer.** A seller asked to sign a full asset purchase agreement on top of a marketplace’s form can check which document controls a conflict and whether one promise could be enforced twice under different limits, because filed agreements claim priority over other documents or supersede earlier ones. [^sitestar-agreement-controls] [^jeffs-entire-agreement]

One filed agreement made its provisions control over any conflicting document, while a marketplace-brokered agreement declared itself the entire agreement and superseded earlier written or oral agreements. [^sitestar-agreement-controls] [^jeffs-entire-agreement]

Escrow.com’s General Escrow Instructions claim priority over conflicting agreements and state that terms absent from the escrow instructions do not bind Escrow.com. [^escrow-instructions-control] Changes require execution by every party, including Escrow.com, which retains the right to reject them. [^escrow-supplemental-instructions]

One filed agreement applied an 18-month survival period to representations across the agreement, other transaction documents and closing certificates, with longer survival for fundamental representations. [^luna-survival-all-documents] It capped the seller’s specified representation liability at the escrow amount and made the seller pay only losses exceeding a $50,000 threshold, called a basket. [^luna-cap-escrow-amount] [^luna-basket-excess-only] Where the seller’s promises appear in both the marketplace form and the full agreement, survival and cap terms that reach every transaction document, as that agreement’s survival clause did, keep the same promise from carrying two different limits. [^luna-survival-all-documents]

Related questions address [Can the buyer and seller use their own purchase agreement or an addendum when a marketplace runs the sale?](#marketplace-process) and the buyer’s [standard-form question](/practice-guides/buying-an-app-business/us#marketplace-standard-form).

## Should a seller hand over the code, passwords and the app before or after the escrow money is released? {#handover-before-or-after-release}

**Short answer.** Escrow.com’s instructions for a sale in which a broker is a party place transfer after the buyer’s funds are secured and verified but before payment to the seller, which follows acceptance or expiry of the inspection period. [^escrow-broker-sale-sequence-after-release]

Under its General Escrow Instructions, confirmed receipt by the buyer starts the inspection period. [^escrow-broker-sale-sequence-after-release] For domain names, the seller must provide the username, password or authorization code needed for access before funds are released. [^escrow-domain-credentials-before-release]

If the buyer rejects the property, it must return the property before receiving a refund, and failure to return it within the specified period causes payment to the seller. [^escrow-return-before-refund] Apple states that a completed app transfer removes the app from the seller’s App Store Connect account. [^apple-transfer-complete-notice]

A negotiated sequence can distinguish inspection readiness from final transfer: one filed marketplace-brokered agreement treated migration as completed when enough assets had moved for the buyer to assess revenue and allowed remaining assets to move afterward. [^jeffs-later-asset-transfers]

## What does a seller actually do to transfer the app, the payment account and the code repository? {#seller-transfer-steps}

**Short answer.** Each asset moves through its own provider’s process, and Apple, GitHub (for a transfer to another personal account) and Google Play each wait for the receiving side to accept or approve before the transfer completes. [^apple-accept-sixty-days] [^github-accept-one-day] [^play-request-review]

| Asset | What the seller does | What the buyer must do or accept | Timing note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| App Store app | Backs up all information about the app first, because the app is removed from the seller’s account after transfer. [^apple-backup-before-transfer] For automatically renewing subscriptions, generates and shares an app-specific secret before initiating transfer. [^apple-shared-secret-initiator-transfer-steps] | Accepts within 60 days of initiation. [^apple-accept-sixty-days] | Completion after acceptance can take up to two business days. [^apple-accept-processing] |
| Google Play app | Submits from an active, registered account; reports do not transfer, and Google suggests downloading needed reports. [^play-accounts-active] [^play-reports-do-not-transfer] | Has an active, registered account and reviews and approves the request. [^play-accounts-active] [^play-request-review] | Support reviews and replies within two business days. [^play-request-review] |
| GitHub repository | Transfers ownership and is then added back as a collaborator; the purchase agreement can require that access to be removed once the buyer has control. [^github-original-owner-collaborator] | Can administer the transferred repository immediately. [^github-new-owner-administers] | An invitation to another personal account expires without acceptance within one day. [^github-accept-one-day] |
| Vercel hosting project | Transfers as an owner of the sending team. [^vercel-team-roles] | Adds the person making the transfer as a member of the receiving team. [^vercel-team-roles] | Both roles must be in place before the transfer starts. [^vercel-team-roles] |
| Stripe payment account | Contacts Stripe Support to confirm required information changes. [^stripe-business-sale-contact-transfer-steps] | Holds the Administrator or Super Administrator role so that ownership can pass to the buyer. [^stripe-owner-to-admin] | Support contact comes first for a business-sale transfer. [^stripe-business-sale-contact-transfer-steps] |
| Domain name | Provides the access username, password or authorization code. [^escrow-domain-credentials-before-release-transfer-steps] | Receives the credentials needed for access. [^escrow-domain-credentials-before-release-transfer-steps] | Delivery precedes escrow release. [^escrow-domain-credentials-before-release-transfer-steps] |

## Why might a seller need to keep the old app project running after closing, and who pays during that period? {#bridge-period-after-closing}

**Short answer.** Copies of the app already on users’ devices can keep relying on the seller’s set-up until the buyer ships an update, so the agreement can give the seller’s continued support a defined end, as one filed agreement did with a transition period of at most 90 days. [^apple-keychain-until-update] [^apple-pay-merchant-id] [^sitestar-transition-ninety-days]

Apple states that keychain sharing continues only until the app is updated. [^apple-keychain-until-update] Push-notification certificates remain valid until expiry, after which the recipient team needs a new certificate. [^apple-push-certificates]

An Apple Pay merchant identifier does not transfer with the app, although transactions continue while the original certificates remain valid, and the buyer needs a new identifier for its first update. [^apple-pay-merchant-id] For hosting moves, Google advises shutting down the old infrastructure only when all users receive content correctly from the new infrastructure and nobody uses the old one. [^google-old-hosting-shutdown]

One filed agreement limited assistance with customer-account transfers and billing to a transition period no longer than 90 days. [^sitestar-transition-ninety-days] Costs follow the account: Amazon Web Services makes an account’s owner responsible for new costs once the account leaves an organization, so a service that stays in the seller’s account during that period bills the seller unless the agreement shifts the cost. [^aws-new-costs-owner-after-closing]

## How can a seller limit its exposure after the sale, including for refunds it no longer controls? {#limit-seller-exposure}

**Short answer.** A purchase agreement can limit specified seller liability through a loss threshold, a cap and a survival period, as one filed agreement did for representation claims. [^luna-basket-excess-only-seller-exposure] [^luna-cap-escrow-amount-seller-exposure] [^luna-survival-all-documents-seller-exposure]

That agreement made the seller liable only for representation losses exceeding $50,000 and capped that liability at the escrow amount, terms explained further in [recovery for inaccurate representations](/practice-guides/asset-purchase-after-closing/us#remedy-allocation). [^luna-basket-excess-only-seller-exposure] [^luna-cap-escrow-amount-seller-exposure] Most representations survived for 18 months after closing, while fundamental representations survived for the applicable statutory limitation periods. [^luna-survival-all-documents-seller-exposure]

One filed marketplace-brokered agreement allocated responsibility for operating liabilities to the seller before completed migration and the buyer afterward. [^jeffs-indemnity-pre-migration]

Google Play keeps pre-transfer orders in the original account, from which refunds can be issued. [^play-transfer-what-moves] Apple leaves the seller access to pre-transfer sales and payment information but excludes later transactions. [^apple-sales-data-split] Both stores split orders and sales data at the date the app transfers, so a refund term tied to orders placed before the app transfer matches what the seller can still see and act on. [^play-transfer-what-moves] [^apple-sales-data-split]

Limits have exceptions: Delaware’s *ABRY Partners V, L.P. v. F & W Acquisition LLC* bars a capped remedy for intentional misrepresentation of a fact stated in the contract. [^abry-lies-not-capped-seller-exposure] California Civil Code section 1668 treats contracts exempting a person from responsibility for that person’s own fraud as contrary to public policy. [^cal-civ-1668-own-fraud]

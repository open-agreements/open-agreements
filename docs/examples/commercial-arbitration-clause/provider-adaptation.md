# Adapting the People’s Court clause to another provider

The [People’s Court model clause](../../../templates/openagreements-cc-by-4.0/openagreements-peoples-court-arbitration-clause/README.md) is drafted for parties who want People’s Court to administer their dispute.
Its open license also permits an adaptation for another provider.
Create and review a separate adapted clause; the People’s Court template’s tier enum is not a selector for AAA or JAMS.

## General clause structure

The following provider-neutral starting point can be adapted to a negotiated commercial agreement.
Complete every bracketed term and reconcile the result with the provider’s rules and the rest of the agreement before use.
It is a manual drafting example, not a programmatic fill configuration for the People’s Court template.

> The parties agree to resolve by binding arbitration disputes between them arising from or relating to this Agreement, including its interpretation, performance, breach, or termination, except to the extent applicable law prevents a claim from being subject to a required arbitration agreement.
>
> [Administrator] will administer the arbitration under [name of rules], available at [rules URL], using [agreed rule-version mechanism]. The tribunal will consist of [supported composition and appointment method]. The juridical seat is [city, state, country], and the language is [supported language]. [Add the agreed procedure, notices, and any required disclosures.]
>
> An award is binding subject to any review provided by the agreed rules and applicable law. The parties consent to entry of judgment on the operative award by a court with jurisdiction, subject to any valid judicial-venue agreement. This clause does not waive a right that applicable law makes nonwaivable. The Federal Arbitration Act governs to the extent it applies; otherwise, applicable arbitration law governs.

## Administration examples

These examples identify possible administrators and rule sets for an adaptation, without ranking them or representing that any provider has approved the resulting clause.
Sources were checked on October 15, 2026.

| Administrator | Rules and drafting sources | Example choices to review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| American Arbitration Association (AAA) | [Commercial resources](https://www.adr.org/commercial/) and [clause drafting](https://www.adr.org/clause-drafting/) | Expressly identify the applicable Commercial Arbitration Rules and Mediation Procedures, supported tribunal composition, seat, and the Rules’ governing-version mechanism. Any optional appeal procedure needs a separate choice. |
| JAMS | [Clause workbook](https://www.jamsadr.com/clauses) and [Comprehensive Arbitration Rules and Procedures](https://www.jamsadr.com/rules-comprehensive-arbitration) | An example could select the Comprehensive Rules, one neutral arbitrator, an agreed seat and language, and the version applicable under Rule 3. Rule 34 addresses an optional appeal procedure, which is not automatically elected by naming JAMS. |

## Changes required for an adaptation

Replace the administrator and all provider-specific references, including the document’s operative title, rule name and URL, version mechanism, seat, language, appointment, notices, filing endpoint, and support details.
Review jurisdiction and arbitrability delegation as an express drafting decision under the selected rules and law.
Remove the People’s Court AI-tier election and proprietary-technology provisions unless the replacement provider offers the identified procedure and the parties expressly choose suitable replacement language.
Do not represent AAA or JAMS as offering People’s Court’s AI-only or AI-assisted human tiers.
Reconsider appeal, correction, finality, judicial venue, confidentiality, publication, fees, and any provisional-relief or funds provisions against the selected provider’s actual rules.
Check whether the provider will accept the dispute and whether a separately drafted fallback is appropriate if it will not.
A name substitution alone does not complete these decisions.

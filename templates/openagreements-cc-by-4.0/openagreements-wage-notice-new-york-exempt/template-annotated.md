## Notice and Delivery

### Delivery and Retention

Provide this notice at hiring. Give written notice of changes at least seven calendar days before the change unless the change is reflected on the wage statement, as provided in Labor Law § 195(2). If the employee’s primary language is not English and the Department of Labor provides a form in that language, use its official bilingual notice and acknowledgement. Otherwise, record the primary language and the applicable English-only basis below. Give the employee a signed copy and retain the signed acknowledgement for six years.

### Selecting the Appropriate Form

This independently authored form follows the subject matter of NYDOL LS59. Confirm applicable duties and salary requirements under both state and federal law; New York’s executive and administrative exemptions have regional salary thresholds above the federal floor. Check current requirements at https://dol.ny.gov/minimum-wage-frequently-asked-questions. It does not determine overtime eligibility or replace a required translation. For an employee who is not overtime-exempt, use the appropriate NYDOL pay notice, such as LS54 for an hourly employee or LS56 for a weekly salary covering a fixed number of hours. Official forms and translations are available at https://dol.ny.gov/notice-pay-rate and https://dol.ny.gov/LS59-doc.

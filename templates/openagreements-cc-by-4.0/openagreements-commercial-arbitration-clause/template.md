## Award and court relief

An award is binding subject to any review provided by the Rules and applicable law. The parties consent to entry of judgment on the operative award by a court with jurisdiction, subject to any valid agreement on judicial venue, including one incorporated through the Rules. A party may seek provisional judicial relief in aid of arbitration in a court permitted by that venue agreement and applicable law. This clause does not waive a review or challenge available under the Rules or a right that applicable law makes nonwaivable.

## Arbitration law

The Federal Arbitration Act governs this clause and the arbitration to the extent it applies; otherwise, applicable arbitration law governs. This clause does not change the substantive law selected elsewhere in this Agreement.

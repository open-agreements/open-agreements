# People’s Court Model Arbitration Clause

## Binding arbitration administered by People’s Court

Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this agreement, or the breach thereof, shall be resolved by binding arbitration administered by People’s Court (https://peoplescourt.ai), a service of Epistemic Labs Inc., under its Rules of Procedure & Evidence in effect when the case is filed. The juridical seat is Wilmington, Delaware, regardless of any party’s residence or the physical or virtual location of the proceedings. The arbitration agreement and arbitration are governed by the Federal Arbitration Act, 9 U.S.C. §§ 1 et seq.

## Tribunal authority

The parties delegate to the People’s Court Tribunal authority to decide the formation, validity, enforceability, scope, and arbitrability of this arbitration agreement. This arbitration agreement is separable from the remainder of this agreement.

## Express agreement to the selected procedure

The parties expressly agree to {procedure_election}.

In the AI-only tier, an AI Tribunal decides and serves the first-instance Award without human merits review before service, except that a human Arbitrator may decide a question the AI Tribunal cannot. In the AI-assisted human tier, an AI panel assists with a draft; the assigned human Arbitrator decides, may revise or reject the draft, and issues the Award in the Arbitrator’s own name.

Any human appeal is limited, proceeds on the existing Record, and follows the deferential standards in the Rules. Every party must confirm the tier by accepting the same exact case-opening procedure and fee terms. This clause contains no waiver of the internal human appeal.

## Proprietary adjudication technology

The parties acknowledge that People’s Court uses proprietary AI adjudication technology that is not subject to disclosure except as required by applicable law. If disclosure is required, the parties consent to appropriate protective or confidential treatment.

## Award and judicial proceedings

Subject to any timely correction, additional-Award request, permitted internal appeal, and nonwaivable judicial rights, the operative Award is final and binding, and judgment may be entered on it in any court having jurisdiction. Judicial proceedings remain subject to the valid venue agreement incorporated through Rule 12.3.

## Commencement and electronic notice

A party may initiate an arbitration at https://peoplescourt.ai/intake. The parties consent to electronic notice in accordance with the Rules, including by email and through the People’s Court Case Page where applicable. The Rules are available at https://peoplescourt.ai/rules. Filing support is available at contact@peoplescourt.ai.

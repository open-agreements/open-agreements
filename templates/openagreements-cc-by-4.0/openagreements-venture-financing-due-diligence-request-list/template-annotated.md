### C.1 Capitalization

### C.10 Section 83(b) elections

### C.11 Section 409A valuations

Provide all Section 409A valuation reports.

### C.12 Conversion schedule

Provide a holder-by-holder schedule of convertible securities converting in this financing, showing the outstanding amount, conversion price, and resulting number and class of shares for each holder. Identify any proposed amendments needed for conversion.

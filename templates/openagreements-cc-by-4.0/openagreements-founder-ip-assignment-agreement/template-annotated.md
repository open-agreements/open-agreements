## Standard Terms

### Background and Consideration

Before the Company was formed, or before the Founder signed an invention assignment agreement with the Company, the Founder created code, designs, content and other materials for the Business listed in Cover Terms. The Company and the Founder intend that the Company own those materials so that the Company holds the rights to its own products and brand. In exchange for the Consideration listed in Cover Terms, the Founder agrees to the terms of this agreement.

### Defined Terms

[[Assigned IP]] means (a) the materials listed as Assigned IP in Cover Terms, and (b) all other code, software, designs, graphics, text, images, data, documentation, inventions, discoveries, improvements, know-how, business plans, domain names, social media accounts, trademarks, trade names and other works of authorship and materials that the Founder made, conceived, developed or acquired, alone or with others, before the Effective Date and that relate to the Business, in each case together with all goodwill associated with any trademark or trade name, and all Intellectual Property Rights in them. Assigned IP does not include Excluded IP.

[[Intellectual Property Rights]] means all patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, rights in designs, database rights and other intellectual property rights anywhere in the world, including applications and registrations, the right to claim priority, renewals and extensions, and the right to sue for and keep damages for past, present and future infringement.

[[Excluded IP]] means the intellectual property listed as Excluded IP in Cover Terms.

[[Confidential Information]] means non-public information about the Assigned IP or the Company's business, products, customers, finances or plans, and non-public information of a third party that the Company holds in confidence. Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes public through no fault of the Founder.

### Assignment

The Founder hereby assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in and to the Assigned IP, including all Intellectual Property Rights in it and all rights to sue for and keep damages for past infringement. To the extent the Founder does not own any part of the Assigned IP, for example because a co-founder or someone else contributed to it, the Founder assigns whatever interest the Founder has.

### Moral Rights

To the extent any moral rights, rights of attribution or integrity, or similar rights in the Assigned IP cannot be assigned, the Founder waives them and agrees not to assert them against the Company, its successors and licensees, to the extent permitted by law.

### Excluded IP and License

The Founder keeps ownership of the Excluded IP. If any Excluded IP is incorporated into, or needed to use, a Company product, service or process, the Founder grants the Company a non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, fully paid-up, transferable and sublicensable license to use, reproduce, modify, distribute, display, perform, make, sell and otherwise exploit that Excluded IP as part of, or in connection with, that product, service or process and its derivatives.

### Delivery and Transfer of Accounts

Within thirty days after the Effective Date, the Founder will deliver to the Company all tangible embodiments of the Assigned IP in the Founder's possession, including source code, design files and documentation, and will transfer to the Company, or to accounts the Company controls, the code repositories, domain name registrations, social media accounts, app store listings and other accounts that hold Assigned IP.

### Further Assurances

The Founder will sign the documents and take the other actions the Company reasonably requests to perfect, record, register or enforce the Company's rights in the Assigned IP, including assignments for recording with the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office or the U.S. Copyright Office and domain name transfer authorizations. This obligation continues after the Founder stops working with the Company, and the Company will reimburse the Founder's reasonable out-of-pocket expenses for that assistance.

### Power of Attorney

If the Company cannot, after reasonable effort, obtain the Founder's signature on a document described under Further Assurances, the Founder appoints the Company and its authorized officers, other than the Founder, as the Founder's agent and attorney-in-fact to sign and file that document on the Founder's behalf, with the same effect as if the Founder had signed it. This appointment is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

### Confidentiality

The Founder will hold Confidential Information in confidence, will use it only for the Company's benefit, and will not disclose it except as the Company authorizes or as required by law. These obligations continue for as long as the information remains confidential, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law. This agreement does not replace any confidentiality and invention assignment agreement between the Founder and the Company covering work after the Effective Date.

### Protected Disclosures

Nothing in this agreement restricts the Founder from reporting possible violations of law to a government agency or attorney, or from making disclosures protected by whistleblower laws, and the Founder does not need the Company's approval to do so. Pursuant to the Defend Trade Secrets Act (18 U.S.C. section 1833(b)), the Founder will not be held criminally or civilly liable under federal or state trade secret law for a disclosure of a trade secret that is made (a) in confidence to a federal, state, or local government official, either directly or indirectly, or to an attorney, solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or (b) in a complaint or other document filed in a lawsuit or other proceeding, if that filing is made under seal.

### Representations

The Founder represents that, to the Founder's knowledge, (a) the Founder has not assigned, licensed or granted any lien on the Assigned IP to anyone else, (b) no former or current employer, client, university or other person has a claim to the Assigned IP under an employment, consulting, invention assignment or other agreement or policy, except as disclosed in Excluded IP, (c) any third-party material, including open-source software, in the Assigned IP has been identified to the Company, and (d) the Founder has disclosed to the Company anyone else who contributed to the Assigned IP.

### General Terms

This agreement is not an employment agreement and does not change the Founder's stock, vesting or compensation terms. The Company may assign this agreement to an affiliate or to a successor to the business to which it relates; the Founder may not assign it without the Company's written consent. If any provision is unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permitted and the rest of the agreement remains in effect. The Company may seek injunctive relief for a breach of the assignment or confidentiality terms, in addition to any other remedy. This agreement is governed by the law listed in Cover Terms, and disputes will be resolved in the venue listed in Cover Terms. Any amendment or waiver must be in writing and signed by both parties. This agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.

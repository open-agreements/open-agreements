## Standard Terms

### Background and Consideration

The Contractor has provided, or will provide, the Services listed in Cover Terms to the Company under the Services Agreement listed in Cover Terms. The Company and the Contractor intend that the Company own the results of the Services. In exchange for the Consideration listed in Cover Terms, the Contractor agrees to the terms of this agreement.

### Defined Terms

[[Work Product]] means all code, software, designs, graphics, text, images, audio, video, data, documentation, inventions, discoveries, improvements, know-how and other works of authorship and materials that the Contractor makes, conceives, develops or reduces to practice, alone or with others, in performing the Services or otherwise for the Company, from the Work Start Date onward, whether before or after the Effective Date, together with all drafts, versions and source files.

[[Intellectual Property Rights]] means all patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, rights in designs, database rights and other intellectual property rights anywhere in the world, including applications and registrations, the right to claim priority, renewals and extensions, and the right to sue for and keep damages for past, present and future infringement.

[[Background IP]] means the Prior Inventions listed in Cover Terms and any other intellectual property that the Contractor owned or developed before the Work Start Date, or develops outside the Services without using the Company's Confidential Information, and that is not Work Product.

[[Confidential Information]] means non-public information of the Company, or of a third party that the Company holds in confidence, that the Contractor learns or receives in connection with the Services, including source code, product plans, customer information, financial information and the Work Product. Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Contractor, (b) the Contractor knew without a confidentiality obligation before receiving it from the Company, (c) the Contractor rightfully receives from a third party without a confidentiality obligation, or (d) the Contractor develops independently without using Confidential Information.

### Assignment of Work Product

The Contractor hereby assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in and to the Work Product and all Intellectual Property Rights in it. To the extent any Work Product is created after the Effective Date, this assignment takes effect automatically when that Work Product is created. The Contractor will not claim any ownership interest in the Work Product.

### Work Made for Hire

To the extent any Work Product is a work of authorship that falls within a category of specially ordered or commissioned works listed in the definition of "work made for hire" in 17 U.S.C. section 101, the Company and the Contractor expressly agree that it is a work made for hire for the Company. Any Work Product that does not qualify as a work made for hire is assigned to the Company under the assignment above.

### Moral Rights

To the extent any moral rights, rights of attribution or integrity, or similar rights in the Work Product cannot be assigned, the Contractor waives them and agrees not to assert them against the Company, its successors and licensees, to the extent permitted by law.

### Background IP and License

The Contractor keeps ownership of its Background IP. The Contractor represents that the Prior Inventions listed in Cover Terms are, to the Contractor's knowledge, a complete list of the Background IP the Contractor expects to use in the Services. If the Contractor incorporates any Background IP into the Work Product, or the Work Product cannot be used without it, the Contractor grants the Company a non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, fully paid-up, transferable and sublicensable license to use, reproduce, modify, distribute, display, perform, make, sell and otherwise exploit that Background IP as part of, or in connection with, the Work Product and its derivatives. The Contractor will not incorporate any third-party material, including open-source software, into the Work Product without telling the Company in writing what it is and under what license.

### Disclosure and Delivery

The Contractor will promptly disclose the Work Product to the Company and, on request and at the end of the Services, deliver all Work Product, including source code, source files, credentials and account access the Contractor holds for the Company, in the form in which the Contractor maintains it.

### Further Assurances

During and after the Services, the Contractor will sign the documents and take the other actions the Company reasonably requests to perfect, record, register or enforce the Company's rights in the Work Product, including assignments for recording with the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office or the U.S. Copyright Office. The Company will reimburse the Contractor's reasonable out-of-pocket expenses for that assistance and, for assistance after the Services end, pay for the Contractor's time at a reasonable hourly rate.

### Power of Attorney

If the Company cannot, after reasonable effort, obtain the Contractor's signature on a document described under Further Assurances, the Contractor appoints the Company and its authorized officers as the Contractor's agent and attorney-in-fact to sign and file that document on the Contractor's behalf, with the same effect as if the Contractor had signed it. This appointment is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

### Confidentiality

The Contractor will use Confidential Information only to perform the Services, will not disclose it to anyone except personnel or subcontractors the Company has approved who are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as these, and will protect it with at least reasonable care. On request and at the end of the Services, the Contractor will return or delete Confidential Information, except copies the Contractor must keep by law. These obligations continue for as long as the information remains confidential, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.

### Protected Disclosures

Nothing in this agreement restricts the Contractor from reporting possible violations of law to a government agency or attorney, or from making disclosures protected by whistleblower laws, and the Contractor does not need the Company's approval to do so. Pursuant to the Defend Trade Secrets Act (18 U.S.C. section 1833(b)), the Contractor will not be held criminally or civilly liable under federal or state trade secret law for a disclosure of a trade secret that is made (a) in confidence to a federal, state, or local government official, either directly or indirectly, or to an attorney, solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or (b) in a complaint or other document filed in a lawsuit or other proceeding, if that filing is made under seal.

### Representations

The Contractor represents that (a) the Contractor has the right to make the assignment and grant the license in this agreement, (b) the Contractor has not assigned or licensed the Work Product to anyone else and is not bound by any agreement that conflicts with this one, and (c) to the Contractor's knowledge, the Work Product, other than third-party material disclosed to the Company, is original to the Contractor and its approved subcontractors. Before any subcontractor or employee of the Contractor works on the Services, the Contractor will obtain that person's written assignment of their rights in the Work Product, so that the Contractor can assign them to the Company.

### Independent Contractor

The parties intend the Contractor to be an independent contractor. Except to the extent applicable law treats the Contractor as an employee for a particular purpose, including because of a work-made-for-hire agreement, this agreement does not make the Contractor an employee, partner or agent of the Company. This agreement does not change the Contractor's compensation or other terms under the Services Agreement except as stated here.

### General Terms

If this agreement conflicts with the Services Agreement on ownership, licensing or confidentiality of the Work Product, this agreement controls. The Company may assign this agreement to an affiliate or to a successor to the business to which it relates; the Contractor may not assign it without the Company's written consent. If any provision is unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permitted and the rest of the agreement remains in effect. The Company may seek injunctive relief for a breach of the assignment or confidentiality terms, in addition to any other remedy. This agreement is governed by the law listed in Cover Terms, and disputes will be resolved in the venue listed in Cover Terms. Any amendment or waiver must be in writing and signed by both parties. This agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.

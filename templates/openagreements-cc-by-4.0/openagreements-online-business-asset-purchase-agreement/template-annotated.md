### Purchase Price

#### Payment

The Purchase Price is the amount stated in Key Terms, modified only by the adjustment or earnout expressly included in this Agreement. A telephone number supplied only in the change request is not the verification number. An unverified email alone does not change payment instructions.

#### Purchase Price Adjustment

The parties shall calculate and settle the price adjustment under Schedule L (Purchase Price Adjustment). Each party shall provide the records required by those terms and may challenge a calculation through the stated process. #### Earnout

Buyer shall calculate and pay contingent consideration under Schedule M (Earnout). The stated operating obligations, reporting access and dispute process govern the measurement periods.
